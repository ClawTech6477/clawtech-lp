Privacy Policy

StatSlam · Last updated: July 10, 2026

StatSlam operates a content publishing pipeline that posts sports statistics videos to social platforms (YouTube, TikTok, and others) using each platform's official API.

1. What we collect

Our publishing tool stores OAuth access/refresh tokens issued by each platform for the single StatSlam account it manages. We do not collect data from viewers, and we do not access any account other than our own.

2. How we use it

Tokens are used solely to authenticate video upload and publishing requests to the StatSlam account. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising profiling.

3. Data retention & storage

Tokens are stored locally with restricted file permissions and are never transmitted to third parties. They can be revoked at any time via each platform's account settings.

4. Third-party APIs

Sports statistics are fetched from public data providers (e.g. football-data.org, jolpica-f1). These requests do not include any personal or viewer data — only publicly available sports statistics are retrieved.
