What StatSlam does

Sports results are public, but they are published as tables and box scores. StatSlam's job is to turn each meaningful change in those tables into something watchable in half a minute.

1

Watches public data

A scheduled watcher polls open sports data providers for MLB games, football fixtures and scorers, and Formula 1 standings, and detects when a result is final.

2

Builds the video

The changed statistics are rendered into a vertical video — score, team marks, standings movement — with narration and captions generated from the same data.

3

Publishes automatically

The finished video is uploaded to the StatSlam account on each platform through that platform's official API. No human step sits between the result and the post.
